Billing & Safety Rules

  • Launch price is for the first paid month only for eligible new customers; regular pricing starts from the second month.
  • Yearly billing: Pay 12 months upfront and receive 10% off the regular monthly price multiplied by 12. The first-month launch offer does not combine with the yearly discount. Applicable taxes are extra.
  • India pricing: The package price is defined in INR. Show the INR package price first and its USD equivalent for reference. The India checkout target is the INR package price plus applicable GST; no undisclosed currency or gateway surcharge should be added to the customer total.
  • Other countries: Package prices remain INR-defined. Automatically detect the visitor's country and display the USD equivalent outside India. At checkout, show the final supported payment currency, converted amount, applicable taxes and any disclosed payment charges before confirmation.
  • Gateway and settlement rule: NextGen aims to receive the intended INR package amount plus applicable GST in its Indian bank account after settlement. Gateway, FX and processing costs must be included in NextGen's pricing/fee model rather than silently deducted from the customer's stated package price. This target is subject to gateway support, settlement rules and final fee validation.
  • Applicable GST is calculated at checkout and added to the displayed India package price where required by law.
  • AutoPay requires your explicit payment mandate. Renewal notices and cancellation controls must be provided.
  • The ₹1 trial does not automatically convert to a paid plan. You must choose and authorize a subscription.
  • Sensitive, uncertain, complaint-related, copyright-risk or policy-risk content stays pending for manual review.

India: fixed INR package price + applicable GST. Other countries: the same INR-defined package price automatically displayed as an estimated USD equivalent. Final checkout conversion, taxes and any applicable charges must be shown before payment.
